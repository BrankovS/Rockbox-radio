# Changelog

## v0.1.0

Initial preview for the Apple iPod Video 5G and 5.5G running Rockbox 4.0.

- Three offline stations and five custom decade presets using Rockbox's music database.
- Station-specific shuffle, Surprise Start, playback controls, and persistent settings.
- Verified in the iPod Video simulator with tagged MP3s. Physical-device testing remains unverified.
